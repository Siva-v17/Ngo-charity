Moments from the field

What your gift does

A donation to Stackly is divided with care. Once you choose a cause, the money goes to a named project with a budget, a timeline and a local partner. We do not pool funds into a mystery account, and we do not spend more than 6% on administration and fundraising.

Within 60 days you receive a photo update and a short note from the field. For larger gifts we also arrange a call with the project lead so you can ask questions directly. Your dashboard keeps a full history of your giving, your receipts and the projects you have supported.

If a project is fully funded before your gift arrives, we will tell you and offer to redirect it to a similar cause. We never use donations for anything other than what was promised.

Common questions

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. Donations qualify for 50% deduction under Section 80G. A receipt is emailed instantly.

How much goes to programmes?

94% goes straight to programmes. Admin and fundraising together are capped at 6%.

Can I choose where my gift goes?

Yes. Pick a cause when you donate, or choose general fund and we will use it where need is greatest.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Anytime, from your dashboard or by emailing help@stackly.org.

How will I know my gift was used?

Every donation is tied to a project. You receive a photo update and a short field note within 60 days, and the full spend is published in our quarterly report.

Can my company or school get involved?

Yes. We run employee volunteer days, classroom fundraisers and matched-giving programmes. Write to help@stackly.org and our partnerships team will plan something that fits your size and schedule.

Do you accept donations from outside India?

Overseas supporters can give by card. We issue a receipt for every gift and can share the documents your local tax office may ask for.
