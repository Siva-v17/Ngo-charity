Why we write these stories

Our blog is not a marketing channel. It is how we keep a promise to every donor: you should be able to see what happened to your gift, in plain language, with the good and the difficult parts included. Each post is written by the person closest to the work, whether that is a programme officer, a teacher or a volunteer.

You will find impact reports with real numbers, interviews with the families we work alongside, and honest accounts of projects that did not go as planned. We also share practical guides for people who want to fundraise, volunteer or start a similar effort in their own community.

If you have a story to tell, we would love to hear it. Beneficiaries, donors and volunteers have all shaped what we publish, and some of our most read pieces started as an email from a reader.
