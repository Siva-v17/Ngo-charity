What we do

Five programmes, one goal: a safer, healthier start for every child.

Clean water

Borewells, filters and tank repairs for villages. We test water quality twice a year and train local caretakers to keep every system running.

Education

Scholarships, books and after-school tuition. Mentors follow each child from primary school through the board exams.

Hot meals

Daily kitchens serving school children. Menus are planned with nutritionists and cooked by women from the village.

Healthcare

Free health camps and eye surgeries. Doctors screen for vision, dental and nutrition issues and refer serious cases for treatment.

Women’s skills

Tailoring and business training with micro-grants. Graduates receive start-up support and a mentor for their first year.

Volunteering

Give a weekend or lend a professional skill. Teachers, doctors, designers and accountants all find meaningful work with us.

Built to last, not just to launch

Each of our programmes follows the same pattern. We assess a need with the community, agree on a plan, fund the work through local partners and then stay involved long enough to see results. We do not walk away on opening day.

That is why every service includes training and a maintenance plan. Well caretakers learn to repair pumps, kitchen teams learn to manage supplies and teachers receive refreshers each term. When the community can run a project on its own, we step back and move on to the next village.

Results are tracked for at least three years. We measure school attendance, health screenings, water quality, household income and more, and we share the findings openly so supporters can judge our work for themselves.

Common questions

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. Donations qualify for 50% deduction under Section 80G. A receipt is emailed instantly.

How much goes to programmes?

94% goes straight to programmes. Admin and fundraising together are capped at 6%.

Can I choose where my gift goes?

Yes. Pick a cause when you donate, or choose general fund and we will use it where need is greatest.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Anytime, from your dashboard or by emailing help@stackly.org.

How will I know my gift was used?

Every donation is tied to a project. You receive a photo update and a short field note within 60 days, and the full spend is published in our quarterly report.

Can my company or school get involved?

Yes. We run employee volunteer days, classroom fundraisers and matched-giving programmes. Write to help@stackly.org and our partnerships team will plan something that fits your size and schedule.

Do you accept donations from outside India?

Overseas supporters can give by card. We issue a receipt for every gift and can share the documents your local tax office may ask for.
