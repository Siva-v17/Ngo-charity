Built by neighbours, for neighbours

Since 2011 we have turned small donations into schools, wells and kitchens.

Our mission

We believe poverty is solved by access: to water, to school, to care. We fund local partners who already know their communities and publish every expense online each quarter.

Stackly began in 2011 when a retired teacher and a handful of neighbours pooled their savings to dig a single well. Today more than 48,000 donors stand behind work in 320 villages, yet we still run like that first group: small teams, honest accounts and decisions made close to the people affected.

Our values

Transparency

Open books, audited yearly. Our accounts, audit letters and project costs are public, and anyone can ask us to explain a line item.

Dignity

Communities lead every project. Villagers choose the priority, supply labour and own what is built, so help never feels like charity handed down.

Impact

We measure outcomes, not activity. We track school attendance, water quality and child nutrition for years, and we stop programmes that do not deliver.

How we grew

2011

First well in Salem district. It served 60 families and proved that community ownership works.

2015

Opened 20 community kitchens. Local women ran them, cooking daily meals for school children.

2020

Delivered 60,000 relief kits. Food, soap and learning material reached families cut off during lockdowns.

2026

320 villages and counting. Nearly 10,000 children are now in school with our support.

Where every rupee goes

Of every 100 rupees donated, 94 go directly to programmes. Administration takes 4 and fundraising 2. Our board reviews these ratios each quarter, and an independent firm confirms them every year.

Our approach to lasting change

Short-term relief can save a life, but it rarely changes a future. We therefore pair emergency help with long-term building: a well comes with a repair team, a kitchen with a nutrition plan and a scholarship with a mentor who checks in every month.

We also say no. If a village already has a working school nearby, we will not duplicate it. If a request does not match what a community truly needs, we say so honestly and suggest a better use of the funds. That discipline is why donors trust us with their gifts and why our results hold up under audit.

Looking ahead, we plan to reach 500 villages by 2030, double our scholarships and build a permanent training centre for women entrepreneurs. Every one of those goals will be published with a budget, a timeline and a public progress report.

Common questions

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. Donations qualify for 50% deduction under Section 80G. A receipt is emailed instantly.

How much goes to programmes?

94% goes straight to programmes. Admin and fundraising together are capped at 6%.

Can I choose where my gift goes?

Yes. Pick a cause when you donate, or choose general fund and we will use it where need is greatest.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Anytime, from your dashboard or by emailing help@stackly.org.

How will I know my gift was used?

Every donation is tied to a project. You receive a photo update and a short field note within 60 days, and the full spend is published in our quarterly report.

Can my company or school get involved?

Yes. We run employee volunteer days, classroom fundraisers and matched-giving programmes. Write to help@stackly.org and our partnerships team will plan something that fits your size and schedule.

Do you accept donations from outside India?

Overseas supporters can give by card. We issue a receipt for every gift and can share the documents your local tax office may ask for.
