Small gifts, lasting change

We work alongside village leaders, not over them. Each project is chosen by the community, built with local labour and checked by independent auditors.

A well is only useful if someone maintains it, and a school only works if teachers stay. That is why each project comes with a local committee, a small maintenance fund and a three-year follow-up plan. We stay until the community says it no longer needs us.

Where your money goes

Hot meals

Rs 30 feeds a child for a day. Our community kitchens cook fresh, locally sourced meals with dal, vegetables and millets, so children arrive at school ready to learn instead of distracted by hunger.

Schooling

Rs 6,000 funds a year of school. It covers uniforms, books, fees and after-school tuition, and keeps a first-generation learner in class through the exam years when most children drop out.

Clean water

Rs 1,200 brings water to a family. We install borewells, hand pumps and household filters, then train residents to test and repair them so the water stays safe long after we leave.

How we work

Every Stackly project begins with a conversation, not a blueprint. Our field teams spend weeks in a village meeting parents, teachers, farmers and panchayat leaders. They ask what is missing, what has failed before and what the community is willing to take ownership of. Only then do we draw up a plan and a budget.

Local partners carry out the work. We hire masons, cooks, tutors and drivers from nearby, which keeps money circulating in the village and builds skills that last. Our engineers and programme staff visit every site, check quality against a written standard and photograph the progress, so supporters can follow it from start to finish.

When a project is complete, an independent auditor reviews the spending and the results. We publish the findings, including what went wrong, in our quarterly report. Learning from our mistakes in public is how we have grown from one well in Salem district to 320 villages across Tamil Nadu.

Common questions

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. Donations qualify for 50% deduction under Section 80G. A receipt is emailed instantly.

How much goes to programmes?

94% goes straight to programmes. Admin and fundraising together are capped at 6%.

Can I choose where my gift goes?

Yes. Pick a cause when you donate, or choose general fund and we will use it where need is greatest.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Anytime, from your dashboard or by emailing help@stackly.org.

How will I know my gift was used?

Every donation is tied to a project. You receive a photo update and a short field note within 60 days, and the full spend is published in our quarterly report.

Can my company or school get involved?

Yes. We run employee volunteer days, classroom fundraisers and matched-giving programmes. Write to help@stackly.org and our partnerships team will plan something that fits your size and schedule.

Do you accept donations from outside India?

Overseas supporters can give by card. We issue a receipt for every gift and can share the documents your local tax office may ask for.
